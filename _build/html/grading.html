Grading

Grading#

Our grading system is different from the traditional system, and is called Standards-Based Grading (SBG), also called Mastery Grading. In SBG, you will be assessed on your mastery of specific learning objectives rather than on a cumulative point system. You will have multiple opportunities to demonstrate your understanding of the material, and your final grade will reflect your highest level of achievement.

There are a lot of benefits to this system, including:

  • You can focus on mastering the material rather than worrying about accumulating points.

  • You will receive feedback on your progress throughout the semester, allowing you to identify areas where you need to improve.

  • You will have a clear understanding of what is expected of you in terms of learning objectives.

You will have to demonstrate knowledge on each Student Learning Objective, and that both in a Quiz and in a Practice Report. The grades with follow a scale of “N” (No Evidence Yet), “P” (Proficient), or “A” (Achieved).

For example:

If you take a quiz and score a “P” on WRA01, you have demonstrated proficiency in that learning objective related to a quiz. If you later take another quiz and score an “A” on WRA01, your grade for that objective will be updated to “A”. If you score an “N”, it means you have not yet demonstrated proficiency, and you can retake the quiz or practice assignment to improve your score.

In the same way, if you submit a practice report and score an “A” on WRA01, it means you have demonstrated mastery of that learning objective in a practical context.

In the page Your Progress, you can see your progress on each learning objective, and the highest grade you have achieved for each one. Something like this:

Final grades will be the percentage of learning objectives you have achieved. To that, consider “A” as 1 point, “P” as 0.5 points, and “N” as 0 points. Project SLOs will be weighted 3x more, as they are more complex and require a deeper understanding of the material.

So, in the end, your final grade will be calculated as follows:

\[ \frac{ \bigl(\#A_{\text{content}} + 0.5 \cdot \#P_{\text{content}}\bigr) + 3 \cdot \bigl(\#A_{\text{project}} + 0.5 \cdot \#P_{\text{project}}\bigr) }{ \text{Number of content SLOs} + 3 \cdot \text{Number of project SLOs} } \]
